❤️ CARDIOLOGY ICMR Standard Protocol

Acute Coronary Syndrome & STEMI Emergency Management

Step-by-step guideline for hyperacute T-waves, ST-segment elevation, MONA protocol (Morphine, Oxygen if SpO2 < 90%, Nitrates, Aspirin 300mg + Clopidogrel 300mg loading dose), and door-to-needle thrombolysis timing.

⚡ Clinical Pearl: Avoid nitrates if right ventricular infarction suspected (leads V3R, V4R) or systolic BP < 90 mmHg.
Difficulty: Intermediate
🫁 PULMONOLOGY GOLD & GINA Guidelines

COPD Exacerbation & Hypoxia Titration Algorithm

Target SpO2 titration: 88-92% in CO2 retainers (prevent loss of hypoxic drive). Salbutamol + Ipratropium nebulization, systemic corticosteroids (Prednisolone 40mg for 5 days), and non-invasive ventilation (BiPAP) indications.

⚡ Clinical Pearl: In severe COPD, high-flow 100% O2 can worsen hypercapnia due to Haldane effect and V/Q mismatch.
Difficulty: Essential
🩸 ENDOCRINOLOGY ADA / RSSDI India

Type 2 Diabetes Glycemic Control & SGLT2i / GLP-1 Algorithm

First-line Metformin titration. In patients with established ASCVD or CKD (eGFR > 20), add Empagliflozin/Dapagliflozin early for cardio-renal protection. Hypoglycemia rule of 15 (15g simple carbs, re-check in 15 mins).

⚡ Clinical Pearl: Hold SGLT2 inhibitors 48 hours prior to scheduled major surgery to mitigate euglycemic DKA risk.
Difficulty: Daily Practice
🫘 NEPHROLOGY KDIGO 2024 Guidelines

Acute Kidney Injury (AKI) Staging & Hyperkalemia Emergency

KDIGO Stage 1 to 3 based on serum creatinine rise & urine output. Urgent Hyperkalemia cocktail (K+ > 6.5 mEq/L): 10ml Calcium Gluconate 10% IV (cardioprotection) + 10 units Regular Insulin in 50ml 50% Dextrose + Salbutamol nebs.

⚡ Clinical Pearl: Calcium gluconate stabilizes cardiac myocytes immediately but does NOT lower serum potassium; insulin-glucose shifts K+ into cells within 15-30 min.
Difficulty: Advanced
👶 PEDIATRICS IAP National Immunization

Pediatric Febrile Illness & Acute Dehydration (WHO Plan A/B/C)

Weight-based paracetamol (15 mg/kg every 4-6h, max 60 mg/kg/day). WHO low-osmolarity ORS rehydration protocol: 75 ml/kg over 4 hours for moderate dehydration. Avoid aspirin in children due to Reye Syndrome risk.

⚡ Clinical Pearl: In neonates (< 28 days) with temperature > 38°C (100.4°F), full sepsis workup and hospitalization is mandatory even if appearing well.
Difficulty: Essential
🚨 EMERGENCY CARE AHA / ERC ACLS 2025

Anaphylaxis & Shock Resuscitation Protocol

First-line drug is Intramuscular Adrenaline (Epinephrine) 1:1000 (0.5mg for adults, 0.01mg/kg for pediatrics) into anterolateral mid-thigh. Never delay epinephrine for antihistamines or steroids. Rapid IV crystalloid bolus 20ml/kg.

⚡ Clinical Pearl: Subcutaneous absorption of adrenaline is erratic in shock; the vascularity of vastus lateralis (mid-thigh IM) ensures rapid peak plasma levels.
Difficulty: Critical Lifesaving
💊 CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY National List of Essential Medicines (NLEM)

Antibiotic Stewardship (AWaRe Classification) & Critical Drug Interactions

WHO Access, Watch, Reserve antimicrobial guidance to prevent Superbug AMR. Major clinical interactions: Clarithromycin/Erythromycin + Statins (Rhabdomyolysis risk), ACE inhibitors + Spironolactone (Severe Hyperkalemia).

⚡ Clinical Pearl: Always verify GFR clearance prior to dosing Amoxicillin-Clavulanate, Fluoroquinolones, and Gabapentinoids in elderly patients.
Difficulty: All Specialties
