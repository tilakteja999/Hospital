National Digital Health Infrastructure

Hospital Government of India Telemetry Portal

This portal connects citizens directly to Government Hospitals across India. It provides real-time personal health telemetry (BP, SpO2, Blood Sugar, Weight, Hemoglobin, Heart Rate), OPD appointments, digitized visit records, tablet schedules, and a multi-lingual Voice AI Assistant to assist every citizen regardless of literacy.

🧭 Guide: What Each Icon & Tab Does

📊

1. Home

Displays your 6 Key Health Vitals (Blood Pressure, Oxygen SpO2, Sugar %, Body Weight, Blood Hemoglobin, and Heart Rate) with interactive trend charts and healthy normal ranges.

📋

2. Records

Chronological list of all visited government hospitals. Clicking any record reveals full doctor notes, diagnoses, and lab reports with a 1-click Sync Vitals button.

📅

3. Bookings

Select your location (auto-detect or type), popular area, hospital dropdown, doctor, and date. Instantly checks live capacity and gives an Accepted or Denied popup.

🔔

4. Notifications

Alerts for scheduled health checkups, medicine refill reminders, seasonal vaccination drives, and Ministry of Health advisories.

💊

5. Prescription

Detailed tablets schedule with Morning (सुबह), Afternoon (दोपहर), and Night (रात) badges, before/after food instructions, and doctor dosage.

🎙️

6. Voice AI Assistant

Omnipresent voice assistant that listens in your language (Hindi, English, Telugu, Tamil, etc.), explains your medical reports calmly without panicking, and automates tab switching.

⚙️

7. Settings

Manage your Aadhaar verification, ABHA Health ID, emergency contacts, high-contrast display mode, and portal language preferences.

📞

8. Emergency Helplines

108: National Ambulance Service
102: Mother & Child Ambulance
1075: National Health Helpline Toll-Free
