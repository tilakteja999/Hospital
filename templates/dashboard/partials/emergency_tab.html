🩺 Quick First Aid Tips

❤️ Cardiac Arrest
Tilt head, 30 chest compressions : 2 rescue breaths. Keep going until help arrives.
🩸 Severe Bleeding
Apply firm, direct pressure with a clean cloth. Elevate the injured limb. Do not remove the cloth.
🧠 Stroke — F.A.S.T.
Face drooping · Arm weakness · Speech slurred · Time to call 112 immediately.
🫁 Choking
Bend person forward, 5 firm back blows. Then 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich). Repeat.
